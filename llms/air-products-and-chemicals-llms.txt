# Air Products and Chemicals

> Air Products and Chemicals, Inc. is a global industrial gases company founded in 1940 and headquartered in Allentown, Pennsylvania. It is one of the world's largest producers of hydrogen and helium, supplying atmospheric, process and specialty gases along with gas-generation equipment, liquefaction technology and engineering services. Air Products publishes no public developer API, no OpenAPI or other machine-readable contract, and no developer portal. Customer and supplier integration is handled through gated portals and bilateral agreements.

## What an agent can and cannot do here

- There is no public API, no API key, no developer signup and no documented base URL. Do not attempt to construct one.
- api.airproducts.com resolves to a live Axway (Vordel) API Gateway. Every path, including the root, returns HTTP 404 with a SOAP 1.2 `fault:MessageBlocked` fault. It is a partner/B2B surface, not a public one, and it publishes no WSDL.
- Customer ordering and tank telemetry live behind the MyAirProducts login at account.airproducts.com; access is provisioned as part of a gas supply agreement, not self-service.
- Supplier purchase-order and invoice exchange is onboarded by email through a Supplier Portal or the SAP Ariba Network. No EDI implementation guide or message set is published.
- The correct next step for an integration is the commercial contact route, not a technical one.
